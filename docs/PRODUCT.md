# NOVE — Product Context

## Surface
Marketing website for NOVE, a company in Marabá, Pará focused on synthetic grass, society arenas, flooring/revestments, and surface maintenance.

## Primary audience
- Homeowners and residential project decision makers.
- Companies and commercial spaces that need durable, presentable surfaces.
- Operators or investors evaluating a society arena.

## Primary visitor job
Understand what NOVE actually solves, develop confidence in the quality of execution, then request an analysis/orçamento for a real space.

## Positioning
NOVE is not presented as a seller of synthetic grass. The product is the transformation of a surface into a space that works better, looks intentional, lasts, and is prepared for real use.

## Conversion
Primary action: request an orçamento / project analysis.
Secondary action: inspect solutions, arena process, and available project references.

## Truth constraints
- Never invent clients, projects, project counts, testimonials, awards, revenue, years of experience, or performance statistics.
- The public portfolio can only show real NOVE work once supplied.
- Until a real WhatsApp number is supplied, the briefing flow copies a ready-to-send message and sends the visitor toward the verified Instagram @nove.br.

## Geographic context
Marabá and surrounding region, Pará, Brazil.
